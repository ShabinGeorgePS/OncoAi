## 🚨 EMERGENCY - When to Call 911

Contact emergency services IMMEDIATELY if you have:
- ✋ Severe bleeding from mouth that won't stop
- 🚫 Difficulty breathing or choking
- 🤕 Severe facial trauma
- 🌡️ High fever with severe mouth pain
- 🫀 Chest pain or severe shortness of breath

## 📋 Self-Examination Guide

Once a month, examine your mouth for:

1. **White or red patches** that don't go away
2. **Lumps or bumps** you can feel
3. **Ulcers/sores** lasting >2 weeks
4. **Pain** when swallowing or eating
5. **difficulty opening mouth** or jaw problems
6. **Numbness** in tongue or lips
7. **Bleeding** with no clear cause

**If you find any of these:** Schedule dentist appointment within 1-2 weeks
